Profit Calculation

Principle-Based vs Fee-Based

Two ways TechCoop recognizes profit on the same goods-and-money flow — one books gross trading margin, the other books a service fee on a pass-through. Pick the right lens, then dive into the schema, FX timing, and reconciliation pages.
Model A
Principle-Based
TechCoop takes title to the goods. Buys from the supplier, sells to the customer. Revenue is the full sale price; cost is the purchase price. Profit is the gross trading margin.
revenue  = sale_price × qty
cost     = buy_price × qty
profit = revenue − cost − fx_adj − fees
Use when TechCoop bears price/inventory/FX risk, issues its own sales invoice, and the customer's contract is with TechCoop.
Model B
Fee-Based
TechCoop acts as agent. Money and goods pass through; only the service fee is recognized as revenue. The supplier–customer contract is the principal transaction.
gmv     = sale_price × qty  // not revenue
fee     = gmv × fee_rate (or flat)
profit = fee − fx_adj − processing_costs
Use when TechCoop never holds title, isn't on the hook for defects/returns, and the customer pays the supplier (with TechCoop facilitating).

1 · Two ways of saying "outstanding"

Same word, two different recipes

Worked example: TC pays a vendor 1 billion VND on the customer's behalf. The customer hasn't paid TC back yet. What does each system call "outstanding"?

farmnet says: 1.07 B VND

What TC paid the vendor1.00 B
+ profit margin (~ 5 %)+ 0.05 B
+ VAT on the margin (~ 2 %)+ 0.02 B
= what farmnet records as outstanding1.07 B

In plain words: farmnet records the customer-facing price (cost + TC's profit + VAT) as the outstanding. The principal, profit, and VAT are combined into one number at SO creation — there's no way to see the parts after the fact.

salefarmgate says: 1.00 B VND  (+ ~33 M fee, separate)

What TC paid the vendor1.00 B
− What the customer paid back so far− 0.00 B
outstanding (principal only)1.00 B
profit_sharing (separate fee, accrues over time)+ 0.033 B

In plain words: salefarmgate's outstanding is the simple cash gap — money out minus money in. The profit-share fee lives in a separate column that grows month by month. To get the full picture, you read both fields.

The structural difference, in one line

farmnet bundles principal + margin + VAT into "outstanding". salefarmgate splits them. A 1 B VND outstanding in farmnet ≈ 935 M VND principal in salefarmgate terms. Comparing the two numbers directly leads to the wrong answer.

2 · farmnet — what's inside the markup

The "+ markup" baked into outstanding has a lifecycle

Set at SO creation as a profit margin. Optionally reduced if the customer pays early. The customer sees only the final number — the journey is invisible.

The three margin states (typical SO)

Margin at SO creation
≈ 5 %
Margin kept if customer pays early
≈ 3 %
Realized markup on the bill
≈ 7 %  (margin + VAT on top)

Stored as two fields on the SO: profit_sharing (gross margin set at creation) and market_development (kept margin after the early-payment rebate). The realized 7 % is slightly above the gross margin because VAT applies on top.

Coverage of the rebate program (4,919 active SOs)

SOs with a profit margin set at creation4,721 (96 %)
SOs eligible for the early-payment rebate395 (8 %)
SOs that actually received the rebate1,485 (30 %)
Total rebate paid out1.5 B VND
Why this matters for "outstanding"

Outstanding starts at disbursement × (1 + 5 %) and may drop to disbursement × (1 + 3 %) after a rebate event. Neither the original margin, the rebate amount, nor the kept margin are exposed as separate fields on outstanding — there's only the current repayment_amount and farmlink_outstanding. A customer can't see why they owe what they owe, and a comparison with salefarmgate's principal-only outstanding is invalid.

3 · salefarmgate — outstanding and fees, side by side

Same SQL, two fields: principal vs accruing fee

salefarmgate's outstanding walks the financial ledger as vendor_paid − customer_received. profit_sharing is computed in the same query but lives in its own column.

outstanding — principal only

running_balance += vendor_payment running_balance -= customer_receipt outstanding = max(0, running_balance)

A pure cash gap. No margin, no VAT, no time component. Updates with each new payment/receipt event on the financial ledger.

profit_sharing — time-accruing fee

profit_sharing += outstanding × (percent / 100) × days_elapsed / 30

Charged on the unpaid principal at a per-month rate. Functions as interest. Recorded as a parallel field — never folded into outstanding.

Aggregate split (340 active SOs)

FieldNon-zero rowsTotal (B VND)Meaning
outstanding 111 129.1 Principal cash still owed by the customer
profit_sharing 102 4.24 Accrued fee (≈ 3.3 % of outstanding)
Why the contrast with farmnet matters

To get the same total in salefarmgate that farmnet expresses in a single number, you'd add outstanding + profit_sharing + accrued VAT. salefarmgate forces the reader to reach for two columns; farmnet folds them into one and loses the breakdown. Both numbers are valid; they're answering different questions, and a 1:1 comparison is misleading.

4 · So what — why this matters for the business

The same dashboard, two different stories

If a manager asks "how much do our customers owe us?", the two systems answer the same question with different recipes — and three different business decisions ride on which recipe you trust.

Three business decisions that change depending on which definition you use

Business question Risk if you read farmnet's number naively Risk if you read salefarmgate's number naively
"Are we taking on too much customer credit risk?" Overstates exposure by ~7 % — markup is profit we'd lose, not cash at risk Understates exposure — the fee column is also unpaid customer debt
"What's our gross margin on outstanding trades?" Margin is hidden inside the outstanding figure — invisible until the trade closes Margin is visible in profit_sharing, but it's an accrual, not realized cash
"How much VAT do we owe the tax authority?" VAT is folded into outstanding — no separate line for tax reporting VAT is tracked elsewhere; outstanding doesn't carry it
The headline finding

"Outstanding" is not a comparable metric across the two systems today. Any consolidated TC-wide report that sums farmnet outstanding + salefarmgate outstanding is mixing principal-with-markup and principal-only — and silently overstates customer credit exposure on the farmnet side while missing the accruing fee on the salefarmgate side.
